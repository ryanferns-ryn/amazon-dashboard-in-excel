Amazon India Sales & Profit Dashboard
An interactive business intelligence dashboard designed to analyze Amazon India sales, profitability, product performance, order status, payment methods, fulfilment methods, and geographic performance.

The project aims to present business data in a clear and accessible format, helping non-technical stakeholders understand performance, identify trends, and make informed decisions.

Objectives
Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
Identify high-performing product categories and products.
Monitor order statuses, returns, and cancellations.
Evaluate payment and fulfilment methods.
Compare sales, profit, and order volumes across states.
Present key business metrics through interactive visualizations.

Key Business Questions
Is the business generating sustainable sales and profits?
Which product categories contribute the most to revenue?
Which products are the top sales performers?
How significantly do returns and cancellations affect business performance?
Which payment and fulfilment methods contribute the most to sales and profit?
Which states represent the strongest markets?
Expected Outcomes
The dashboard is designed to help stakeholders:

Monitor business performance through key performance indicators.
Identify monthly sales and profit trends.
Recognize high-performing and underperforming product categories.
Understand the impact of returns and cancellations.
Evaluate payment and fulfilment channel performance.
Identify geographically important markets.
